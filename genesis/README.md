# Project Baby Universe — Genesis Branch

A research programme on the quantum creation of new spacetime regions. It asks whether an independently
expanding daughter universe can originate from a physically admissible parent spacetime through a consistent
gravitational process.

This directory is self-contained and independent of the Q-CHAS dashboard in the rest of the repository.

## Phase III in five lines

1. The two FGG prescriptions are two conventions for the boost angle of a flipped wall normal: complex (Sorkin, $i\pi$) or real.
2. The true Hamiltonian is two-sheeted. The tunnelling trajectory touches its branch locus exactly where the Euclidean wall
   folds in Killing time.
3. The FGG slicing (principal) prescription solves no analytic quantum constraint, and $B<0$ in 33/36 models.
4. **Double-flip transitions:** every consistent quantization gives the same exponent $B_{\rm an}$, so the ambiguity is eliminated there.
5. **Single-flip transitions:** two consistent answers remain, $B_{\rm an}$ if the wall's exterior orientation is superselected and
   $B_{\rm sq}=\frac2G\int R\arccos|C|\,dR<B_{\rm an}$ if not. The universal Stokes multiplier is $\sqrt2$.

## Phases IV–VI in brief

* **IV (fields).** The full Einstein–scalar evolution reproduces the thin-wall picture. Laboratory bubbles collapse to black holes and never
  develop an anti-trapped region (Prop. IV-1, valid for any NEC matter). Both inflating daughter branches exist as field-theory solutions,
  but only from data that already lie beyond a bridge, in a white hole, or beyond a cosmological horizon.
* **V (quantum system).** The reduced quantum theory is a scattering problem. The parent channel ends at $t=\infty$. The daughter channel's finite-$t$ "edge"
  was later shown to be a coordinate singularity (roadmap F16); the daughter needs its own clock because the boundary clock folds back. The daughter is invisible to every exterior observable. The transition preserves spatial topology
  and creates a throat.
* **VI (observability).** Individually undetectable. Laboratory creation: best conceivable case $P\le10^{-46}$, at the edge of validity near the
  Planck scale. The only unsuppressed route is cosmological, with a characteristic black-hole mass $M_*\approx5.6\,{\rm g}\,(10^{16}\,{\rm GeV}/E_{\rm vac})^2$.

## Roadmap round in brief

* **Suppression is a theorem for light walls:** $B_{\rm lab}=B_{\rm CdL}\Phi((R_{\rm CdL}/R_M)^2)\ge4\pi^2\sigma^2R_M^2/\rho$, and
  $B_{\rm lab}\ge1.67\sqrt{B_{\rm CdL}(M/m_P)(R_M/\ell_P)}$. No controlled laboratory transition has $P>10^{-23}$.
* **The FGG ambiguity** exists only for $M>M_{\rm sd}=(\chi^2-\kappa^2)/2G\chi^3$ or $\kappa\ge\chi$. Where it exists, it is the choice of which conserved charge is probability.
* **The daughter** needs no boundary condition. The parent's *Killing* clock runs backwards along it, but every global time function runs forwards
  and the conserved charge is positive (round 3 corrects the earlier "time-reversed daughter" reading, F17).

## Round 6g in brief (creating the region; a machine)

* **Created by the universe.** A first-order transition with supercooling leaves false-vacuum patches. In the project's own GR solver
  (cosmological light-cone data, FRW verified), patches below 0.57–0.59 l_H collapse into black holes and larger ones inflate, behind a
  trapped region, with non-decreasing horizon areas for the parent and the daughter observers. The near-critical patches are the route's ball,
  and the condensate tilt turns collapsing ones into daughters.
* **Not by external assembly.** Delivering a near-Nariai region's energy within one light-crossing needs ~c⁵/(3G) ≈ 10⁵² W (static holding
  ~10⁴³ N): a conditional engineering bound. The Round 6h review withdrew the stronger "no machine of any kind" wording.

## Round 6l in brief (one model with both doors)

* Model U: D = 4 + n Einstein gravity + the Bueno–Cano–Hennigar tower. Regular holes, horizons above μ_ext, T → 0 there (remnants). Matter
  interiors bounce at a = l and accelerate for ((D−3)/(D−1)) ln(R/l) e-folds (29.6 in a 4D stellar hole), then recollapse. Lasting
  inflation needs a vacuum as dense as the hole; vacuum collapse leaves a static core.
* Collider (NNPDF2.3 LO): daughter-bearing holes at 100 TeV iff M_ext ≈ 9–50 TeV, i.e. l M_D ≈ 0.45–0.80 (Hayward) or 0.54–0.95
  (Dymnikova); up to ~10⁹ events. They form 20–7000× faster than they evaporate and leave remnants holding M_ext/M of the mass.
* Multiple daughters: later infall meets the bounced matter inside the same core; after 7–30 l of delay, DTR plus the curvature bound
  saturate the crossing (the Barrabès–Frolov regime, open).
* Experiments: monojets give M_D; the black-hole onset, T(M) and remnants identify the class; the daughter itself is outside J⁻(I⁺).

## Round 6k in brief (the two doors: a conditional yes)

* Y1 in definite theories: ECSK bounce at 3.9×10⁵⁸ kg/m³ (holes > 4.3×10¹⁰ kg); the interior re-expands beyond the horizon and, with vacuum
  energy l_d < 2.6 A, inflates. Limiting curvature joins Schwarzschild(-Tangherlini) to de Sitter at r₀ = (r_s^{n+1}l²)^{1/(n+3)} if r_s > l.
* Y1 + Y2: with n ≥ 4 extra dimensions and M_D ≲ 15 TeV (allowed), black holes above M_Y = 3.9–4.7 M_D (24–68 TeV) hold daughters, below
  the ~71 TeV reach of a 100 TeV collider; they form before evaporation. Semiclassical control needs 10–100 PeV.
* Test of the interior class: static cores leave remnants (T → 0); expanding interiors look like GR outside.
* A primordial black hole as Planet Nine (if real) is ~10 years away by laser-launched probes.

## Round 6j in brief (geometry, stored and supplied energy; fifteen loopholes)

* The invariant is the identity E√ρ̄ = √(3/32π)c⁶G^{−3/2}k^{3/2} at k = 1: a geometric statement and, with ρ ≥ 0, a bound on stored energy.
  It does not bound supplied energy, but a small supplied energy needs a natural precursor that is stable, older than the universe and
  within ~10⁻⁸ of threshold: none exists.
* The density in the theorem is the areal mean of the zero-flux energy density (Misner–Sharp identity, symbolic). Non-uniformity and
  negative energy only make it harder.
* Field-dependent G: the strong-gravity core is itself horizon-class (compactness ≥ (3/4)ln²F). Finite pockets expand by 10⁻¹⁶.
* Accretion, compact precursors and evaporating holes cannot prepare a daughter-ready state with human-scale input.
* Euclidean wormholes: connection costs ΔS = 3π³a₀² (8πG = 1); the throat-free limit is the disconnected no-boundary universe.
* Conditional doors: FMM-type black-hole interiors (every hole a daughter); strong gravity at 6–20 TeV (collider holes are fundamental-scale
  objects; semiclassical interiors need 10–100 PeV).

## Round 6i in brief (seven routes; the bridge invariant)

* Route 1, curvature activation (scalar–Gauss–Bonnet): the Gauss–Bonnet null stress is a total derivative, and activation raises F, so it
  pushes throats toward black holes (Proposition 6i-1). Nonlinear Nariai throats collapse or lose predictivity: 0 daughters in 152 runs.
  Laboratories are 19 orders short of activation.
* Route 2, monopole cores: stable below η_c ≈ 0.25–0.40 m_P (reproduced). The cheap channel (B ≈ 650/λ) ends disconnected from our universe;
  the connected one needs a magnetic black hole (M > Q ≈ 7 m_P) and has B ≥ 3×10³.
* Route 3, breathing bubbles: the hidden case needs NEC-violating dust, and every well crosses zero net surface energy at light speed.
* Route 4: the gauged universal condensate has an empty window (screening). A vacuum-dependent scale M(χ) lowers the background to 9×10⁸ kg
  (conditional).
* Route 5: resonance never removes the barrier next to the inflating branch. Route 6: collision pockets cannot reach the hidden branch in a
  normal parent. Route 7: four-form membranes are Farhi–Guth walls, with B ≈ B_CdL.
* Theorem 6i-11 (bridge invariant): every classical route is a black-hole formation event at E√ρ̄ = √(3/32π)c⁶/G^{3/2} = 2.3×10⁶⁵ J^{3/2}m^{−3/2}.
  The one door no established result closes (Q*, a barely metastable TeV vacuum) has a bridge 10⁻¹⁶ of a Planck length.

## Round 6h in brief (routes A–D; what actually blocks a machine)

* Theorem 6h-1 (Penrose / Farhi–Guth): no machine of NEC-respecting matter can create a daughter from a universe with a regular past, by any
  strategy. The escapes are NEC violation, a cosmological past singularity, or a quantum transition.
* Route A: in a cosmological (anti-trapped) background, a positive-energy trigger of ~10% of a patch's vacuum energy turns collapse into a
  daughter. In a normal background the trigger must violate the NEC.
* Routes B–D: the black-hole-assisted route needs Nariai-class black holes; dynamical assembly with the allowed condensate needs
  ≥ 2.5×10⁹ M_☉; TeV gravity is excluded below 10.1 TeV. A false-vacuum-only condensate would lower the floor (open).

## Round 6e in brief (the three immediate problems)

* **1. The condensate makes the pulse itself.**
  * Simulated as a dynamical, back-reacting source in the IR limit: geodesic flow, shift charge, a clock.
  * Its own stress tensor tips the throat, with no prescribed flux, and the daughter is hidden.
  * The charge that matters is the charge near the corner, which is where the condensate stays.
  * It is stable on the measured history. In the self-generated case the window is tight, about a factor 3 in M³r_N.
  * Shift symmetry forbids making the charge from nothing (Theorem 6e-1). A soft tilt confined to the false vacuum makes it from zero
    charge, and the complete route closes with that self-made charge.
* **2. Generalised entropy.**
  * Every eternal daughter grown from normal data violates the local GSL, whatever supplies the NEC violation (Theorem 6e-6).
  * The same tilt also ends the daughter's inflation. It exits to Λ = 0 and recollapses inside the parent, and the GSL then holds on every
    causal horizon of a complete observer.
* **3. Creating the ball.**
  * No classical obstruction applies: the data are normal.
  * Heating restores the false vacuum from the true vacuum, and free-streaming cooling leaves an empty, momentarily static ball of radius
    0.6 l_H. Every stage stays normal for strong transitions. **Withdrawn in Round 6f** (invalid free energy; the GR evolution fails).
* **Fixed along the way (F42):** the explicit stiff-scalar step was unstable where m h e^s/2 > 1. The fix is an implicit potential force.
  Unseeded earlier results stand.

## Round 6c in brief (making the throat work)

* **The extremal throat cannot both hide and grow** a daughter (Theorem 6c-1: normal false vacuum is always sub-horizon).
* **The Nariai throat can.** A near-Nariai Schwarzschild–de Sitter hole in a false-vacuum layer, tipped by outgoing negative energy, grows an
  inflating daughter behind a persistent trapped neck. Every run obeys one law: the daughter grows iff the weighted NEC deficit
  $W=-\int Ne^{-u/\tau}du$ exceeds $1.85\times10^{-3}r_N/G$, with $\tau=3.2r_N$.
* **Cost (corrected, F26).** In local energy, 0.55% of the hole mass, 270 times below the flat-space flip. Invariantly, $\sim10^{-2}\sqrt\delta$ of
  the hole mass, bought with tuning $\delta$.
* **Sources.** No QNEC-constrained quantum field can tip it inside the effective theory: that needs $c\ge0.89(r_N/\ell_P)^2$, and vacuum
  polarisation is stabilising. No NEC-respecting field can do it in any parent. A ghost condensate can, 145–430 times below its stability ceiling
  (Lorentz-violating; its GSL is not universal).
* **Preparation.** Possible in theory from normal data with a regular past, if the hole forms inside the false vacuum. The vacuum is tied to the
  hole by $\rho_f^{1/4}=0.329\,{\rm GeV}(M_\odot/M)^{1/2}$ (corrected in Round 6j). There is no laboratory route.
* **Also:** P-S3 solved ($c(0^+)=-13/18$); 3+1 greybody factors (P-R7); GSL-3 without spherical symmetry (given the support form of the
  local GSL).

## Round 6b in brief (getting around the GSL)

* **Correction F21:** NEC violation is not GSL violation; Round 6's "Route G must violate the GSL" is withdrawn.
* **Theorem GSL-3 (dichotomy):** the GSL forbids exactly the eternal (de Sitter-ending) daughter determined by laboratory data, and the
  disconnected one. Transient daughters whose neck persists, and daughters beyond a Cauchy horizon, are GSL-compatible.
* **Theorem GSL-4 (QNEC cost):** anti-trapping costs an entropy-gradient change $S_{\rm BH}\lvert\theta_-\rvert$, about $10^{70}$ for a 1 m sphere,
  discounted by $\sqrt f$ in near-extremal throats. There it is payable when $T\sinh(\rho/Q)<(c/12)T_{\rm brk}$ (verified three ways,
  including an exact Einstein–Maxwell–null-fluid metric).
* **What remains:** growth needs $\Lambda_{\rm loc}Q^2>3/16$ in the throat, the same $M\sim m_P^3/\sqrt\rho$ law as every other route.
  The obstacle is vacuum energy, not the second law.

## Rounds 5, 7 and 8–12 in brief

* **Rounds 8–12.** These rounds are theory only. They contain the GENESIS-1 requirements table for both routes, and the gate tests for Route G, which can be done
