# Round 10: the open problems, and the experimental prototype programme

**Task**
* Work all open problems of the model-U programme.
* Then strengthen the experimental case. Specifically:
  1. a realistic detector simulation;
  2. a profile-likelihood reach with uncertainties;
  3. what the signatures actually establish, with a full statistical treatment;
  4. a reinterpretation of current LHC data with the exact signature.
* Build the Round 10 programme:
  * 10A: event pipeline;
  * 10B: detector and remnant studies;
  * 10C: statistics;
  * 10D: demonstrators and public data.

**Code**
* `doublenull10.py`, `openprob10.py`, `pipeline10.py`, `stats10.py`, `analysis10.py`, `lhcdata10.py`.
* Scripts: `scripts/round10_open.py`, `scripts/round10a.py` (samples), `scripts/round10.py` (analysis), `scripts/machine10.py`,
  `scripts/opendata_monojet.py`.
* Tests: `tests/test_round10.py` (5).
* Data: `DATA/round10_open.json`, `DATA/round10.json`. The event samples are regenerated by `round10a.py` and are not committed.
* Figures: `FIGURES/round10_open.png`, `FIGURES/round10.png`, `FIGURES/machine.png`.

**Headline**
* **The experimental case survives a realistic simulation.** Its numbers move:
  * FCC-hh 100 TeV with 30 ab⁻¹ discovers model U to **M_ext ≈ 33 TeV**, and 85 TeV with 20 ab⁻¹ to **≈ 27 TeV**.
  * These numbers come from a profile likelihood with nuisance parameters, checked against pseudo-experiments, and they are insensitive
    to the background systematics.
* **Today's LHC data exclude** M_ext ≲ 3.5–4.2 TeV (head-on trapping) and ≲ 6.6–7.2 TeV (full trapping). This uses the full
  13-bin ATLAS likelihood.
* **On the theory side**, model U survives with one important qualification and one gain:
  * *Qualification:* its inner horizon becomes a curvature singularity under perturbation, although ψ stays bounded.
  * *Gain:* a bouncing interior with a scalar field reaches lasting inflation by cosmological hysteresis.

## Part I: the open problems

### P-R6n-a: a characteristic (double-null) code for model U. Solved

* **Equations.** The double-null equations for a massless scalar in the quasi-topological tower were derived symbolically:
  * the uu and vv constraints h′(ψ)(r_uu − 2σ_u r_u) = −rφ_u²;
  * r_uv from the first law;
  * σ_uv from preservation of the constraints, with one term reduced by the vv constraint.
* **Checks of the equations.** They reduce exactly to D-dimensional Einstein–scalar theory. The static model-U hole satisfies them
  identically (σ_uv = −f f″/8).
* **Formulation.** In (r, σ) variables, truncation error near the Cauchy horizon is blueshifted, and ψ overshoots the pole (F66).
  The production solver is a hybrid: μ is carried with the first-law fluxes, so ψ = h⁻¹(μ/r^{D−1}) < 1/α by construction.
* **Validation:**
  * the static hole is held to 10⁻⁷ in r at the Cauchy horizon;
  * the Kretschmann scalar matches its exact value 21.23;
  * convergence is second order;
  * constraint violation falls with resolution.
* **Limits:** results are converged to v ≈ 25 l. In D = 10 the run breaks down near v ≈ 10.

### P-R6l-c: stability of the inner horizon. Answered: unstable [Proposition-N 10-1]

A scalar pulse falls into a D = 5 Hayward hole (μ = 10).

**What happens.** Along outgoing rays inside the hole:
* the Kretschmann scalar grows from 23 to ~10⁹ by v = 15 l and to ~3×10¹²–7×10¹² by v = 20–24 l;
* the result agrees between 2000×8000 and 4000×16000 grids to within 5–15 %.

**Where the growth sits.** It is almost entirely in the curvature of the radial–time plane (R₂², driven by e^{−2σ}φ_uφ_v), i.e. the
blueshifted product of influx and outflux. Meanwhile:
* ψ stays below the tower's bound (≈ 0.95/α);
* r stays at r ≈ r₋;
* the mass grows only moderately (×2).

**Interpretation.** The tower bounds the areal curvature ψ, not the full Riemann tensor. The Cauchy horizon becomes a null curvature
singularity with finite area, the structure of Ori's weak singularity.
* Whether it is tidally weak is **Conjecture 10-1**.
* The same happens for the Dymnikova tower and for a 5× smaller pulse.
* This is the refined analysis that the literature called for: Di Filippo, Kubizňák & Srinivasan (2026) showed that the thin-shell
  derivation of mass inflation does not apply to these theories.

**Consequence for the daughter.** In the bouncing-interior picture the daughter lies beyond the inner horizon. Classical
perturbations therefore separate it from the parent's late-time infall by a null curvature singularity. The statement "the daughter
persists" holds for the bounced region. Its continuation through the Cauchy horizon is now an open question about singularity
strength and quantum effects (P-R10-a). The known way around it is an inner-extremal tower (κ₋ = 0; Di Filippo, Kolář & Kubizňák,
PRD 111, L041505 (2025)), but that requires a tuned mass.

### P-R6l-a / P-R6k-c: late infall. Answered

A second pulse at v = 12–16 feeds the existing inner region:
* the mass rises ×9;
* ψ is pushed to 0.99/α;
* the Cauchy-horizon radius shrinks by 3 %.

No separate de Sitter world opens in the evolved domain. This agrees with Proposition 6l-5: later infall enters and modifies the
existing core.

### P-R6l-b: what fixes c_l. Partly answered [Assessment]

* **The link.** The tower h = ψ + αψ² + … contains Gauss–Bonnet with coefficient α = l².
* **The constraint.** Camanho, Edelstein, Maldacena & Zhiboedov (JHEP 02 (2016) 020) show that such a correction violates causality
  unless a tower of higher-spin states exists at m ≲ 1/l.
* **What follows.** Model U is an effective theory whose limiting length also sets a new-physics threshold, M_HS ≲ M_D/c_l
  (1.25–2.2 M_D across the window). The value of c_l is not fixed, but it is tied to a second observable.

### P-R6l-e: a dynamical vacuum through the bounce. Answered: yes, by cosmological hysteresis [Proposition-N 10-2]

**Setup.** A closed dust interior (D = 10) with a scalar field in a false/true-vacuum potential. A fraction ε of the bounce energy is
given to the field, and energy is conserved.

**Single bounce.** Lasting inflation needs V_F > Λ_c(C) (Proposition 6l-2). For a collider-sized hole Λ_c = 0.28/l².

**Many bounces.** Over successive cycles the field's work pumps the cycle maxima, which grow 1.52 → 1.60 → 1.92 → … → 9.5 → 49. The
interior then crosses into the de Sitter branch, and lasting inflation follows for **V_F as low as 0.05 Λ_c**. This is cosmological
hysteresis (Sahni & Toporensky, PRD 85, 123542 (2012)).

**Scan.** Kicked configurations (ε = 10⁻³–10⁻², field value at the bounce in [0, 1], both signs) reach lasting inflation within
t = 600 l in 75–100 % of cases for V_F/Λ_c = 0.05–2, after a median of 0–15 cycles. Two of the 48 runs exhausted the integration budget.

**Controls.** With ε = 0, or with the field's vacuum at V = 0, the interior cycles for ever: 800–960 identical cycles.

**Scope.** Homogeneous interior only. The inhomogeneous and quantum versions are P-R10-b.

### P-R6n-b: rotation. Partly answered [Assessment]

* **Spin of collider holes.** Near threshold they carry large spin (mean a* = 3.3 in the generator).
* **Energy available.** A Myers–Perry proxy gives E_rot ≈ 25 % of M. That energy could be released as visible TeV quanta by
  superradiance, even at T = 0.
* **Treatment.** This is carried as a signal variant in 10A.
  * The discovery channel is robust to it: efficiency rises from 34 % to 66–70 %.
  * MET/S_T is not robust (it falls to ~0.35).
* **Still open.** Rotating model-U solutions are not known.

### P-R9-b: remnant charge. Answered with a baseline and an alternative [Assessment]

* **Rates.** Integrating the Schwinger rate over the field outside r₊:
  * colour discharges in ~0.01 fm/c, about 100× faster than hadronisation;
  * electric charge is removed by e⁺e⁻ emission in ~10⁻²³ s.
* **Baseline: neutral remnants.** The triality–charge correlation of the Standard Model makes a colour-singlet remnant
  integer-charged, and e⁺e⁻ emission then neutralises it.
* **Discharge partons** carry ~0.5–1.4 TeV. They are a signal variant (efficiency 34 % → 38–42 %).
* **Alternative.** If colour discharge were suppressed, the remnant would dress like an R-hadron, with a charged fraction of
  ~0.3–0.5 by flavour counting.

### P-R9-c: the onset as a measurement of c_l. Answered

* **From the rate alone,** with 30 % signal-normalisation uncertainty: δM_ext/M_ext = 2–8 % across the window, i.e. δc_l/c_l =
  0.3–1 %, because M_ext ∝ c_l^{n+1}.
* **Including the shape** (with the JES nuisance), the statistical precision is better than 1 %. That is limited in practice by
  initial-state-radiation modelling, which is not varied here.

### Other items

* **P-R6k-a (complete theory).** Model U is a consistent pure-gravity effective theory. Its UV completion must contain the higher-spin
  tower of P-R6l-b. Open.
* **P-R6k-b (threshold at R_S ≈ l).** Addressed in part by Rounds 6n and 10. The semiclassical caveat remains.
* **P-R6k-d (Planet Nine).** Outside this programme; not pursued.
* **P-R6k-e, P-R6l-d, P-R9-a.** Superseded by 10A.

## Part II: Round 10, the experimental prototype programme

### 10A: event pipeline (`pipeline10.py`)

**Backgrounds.** Pythia 8.3 in pT-hat slices, with shower, hadronisation and decays:
* Z(→νν)+jet;
* W(→ℓν)+jet, ℓ = e, μ, τ;
* tt̄;
* QCD (re-smeared 10× to populate response tails).

**Signal.**
* The model-U generator samples x₁, x₂ and flavours.
* The invisible production system is written to a Les Houches file and showered by Pythia. Initial-state radiation, its hadronisation
  and the recoil all come from the parton shower.
* Hawking quanta (and the discharge and spin-down variants) are added in the hole frame and boosted.

**Detector.** Parametric, after the official Delphes FCC-hh card (M. Selvaggi):
* calorimetry to |η| = 6;
* HCAL 50–100 %/√E ⊕ 3–10 %;
* anti-kT R = 0.4 jets;
* a Gaussian response with a low-side tail (p_tail = 10⁻³) for QCD mismeasurement.

**Pile-up.**
* A minimum-bias library (SoftQCD), overlaid at Poisson(⟨μ⟩): 1000 at FCC-hh, 34 at the LHC.
* Charged pile-up is removed with ε_CHS = 0.95.
* Neutral pile-up is scaled by 0.3 (PUPPI-like) and enters both MET and the jet noise.

**Validation at 13 TeV against ATLAS (139 fb⁻¹).** The same chain, with the exact ATLAS selection as implemented in CheckMATE:
* reproduces the published post-fit background in all 13 MET bins (200 GeV to > 1.2 TeV), with one factor K = 1.18 (NLO/LO-like);
* the residual is **10 % rms**;
* the composition is Z(νν) 70 %, W 28 %, top 2 %, QCD < 0.1 %.

### 10C: statistics (`stats10.py`)

**Likelihood.**
* Binned in MET.
* Log-normal nuisances for each background process. Two scenarios:
  * LHC-like: Z 5 %, W 7 %, top 20 %, QCD 100 %;
  * conservative: Z 15 %, W 20 %, top 30 %, QCD 100 %.
* A JES/MET-scale morphing nuisance (±2 %), luminosity 2 %, signal normalisation 30 %, and per-bin MC statistics.

**Fitting.** Fisher-scoring fits on the analytic Jacobian (F67).

**Checks.**
* The Asimov significance matches the analytic formula to 10⁻¹⁰.
* Pseudo-experiments, with fluctuated global observables:
  * median Z = 3.86 against 3.95 asymptotic;
  * background-only P(q₀ > 1) = 0.17 against 0.159, and P(q₀ > 4) = 0.030 against 0.023.

**Reach** (n = 6, M_D(DL) = 8 TeV, Hayward):

| Machine | 5σ discovery (median) | 95 % CL exclusion (expected) | Effect of the systematics scenario |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 TeV, 30 ab⁻¹ | **M_ext ≈ 33.4 TeV** | 35.0 TeV | < 0.1 TeV |
| 85 TeV, 20 ab⁻¹ | **≈ 26.8 TeV** | 28.0 TeV | < 0.1 TeV |

Why the reach is where it is:
* The remnant recoils only against initial-state radiation, so MET ≈ pT(ISR) ≈ 0.5–1 TeV, independent of M_ext.
* At the top of the window the signal therefore sits on the Z(νν) background at low MET. There, more luminosity no longer helps: with
  15 % background uncertainty the 85 TeV curve stops reaching 5σ beyond ~27 TeV.
* Round 9's 33 / 40 TeV (order-of-magnitude backgrounds) is superseded (F65). The ordering is unchanged and the window is still
  covered.

**Variants** (100 TeV, M_ext = 33.8 TeV, 30 ab⁻¹):

| Variant | Median Z |
|---|---|
| Baseline | 4.0 |
| Colour-discharge partons | 4.2 |
| Spin-down quanta | 17.8 |
| Dymnikova (M_ext = 34.6 TeV) | 2.8 |

Extra visible energy raises the MET acceptance, so the discovery channel does not depend on the unknown visible fraction.

### Item 3: what the signatures establish

**Method.** The exact distribution of the N-event log-likelihood ratio (FFT convolution, no toys needed for 3×10⁻⁷ tails). The
features are MET, jet multiplicity and MET/S_T after the full detector and selection. Backgrounds are mixed in at S/B = 1.

| Hypotheses separated (5σ, median) | Pure signal | S/B = 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Model U vs GR hole at the same threshold | 1 event | 12–13 events |
| Model U vs ADD KK-graviton emission (Pythia LED, same M_D) | 120 | 563 |
| Model U vs vector-mediator dark matter (3 mass points) | 85–93 | 239–367 |
| Model U vs model U with spin-down quanta | 2 | 18 |
| Hayward vs Dymnikova tower | 32 | 170 |

The earlier "3 events vs GR" (Round 7, generic detector, no backgrounds) lies within this range. It is now replaced by the full
calculation.

**What selects model U over the alternatives.** Dark matter or a heavy invisible state can mimic the topology. What they cannot
mimic together is:
1. KK-graviton monojets at low MET that fix n and M_D;
2. an onset of heavy invisible production whose **rate above threshold is predicted** by the geometric cross-section with that n and
   M_D. Only c_l is free, and the onset measures it to ~1 %;
3. a falling-then-rising temperature–mass relation, seen in the minority of events with Hawking quanta (weak);
4. if charged, slow heavy tracks with M = p/(βγ) equal to the onset mass.

A dark-matter mediator has a free coupling and a resonant mass. The rate–onset consistency (2) is therefore the discriminator. The
shape alone needs hundreds of events.

**What stays inaccessible.** None of these observations reaches the daughter. It lies outside J⁻(I⁺). Its continuation through the
Cauchy horizon is now itself in question (P-R10-a). Experiments test the class of the exterior and the remnant, not the interior.

### Item 4: reinterpretation of current LHC data

**Method.** A full likelihood over the 13 exclusive ATLAS MET bins (139 fb⁻¹): observed counts, post-fit background ± error per bin
treated as uncorrelated, and our 13 TeV model-U signal through the same chain.

**Observed 95 % CL lower limits on M_ext** (expected values agree within 0.2 TeV):

| n, M_D(DL) | Head-on trapping | Full trapping |
|---|---|---|
| 6, 4 TeV | 3.5 TeV | 6.6 TeV |
| 6, 2 TeV | 4.2 TeV | 7.2 TeV |
| 4, 4 TeV | 3.9 TeV | 6.8 TeV |

**CMS high-multiplicity search** (arXiv:2604.10732: N ≥ N_min objects, S_T > S_T,min). Model-U acceptance is ≤ 0.4 % for S_T > 2 TeV,
so those limits do not constrain model U. Their limit curves are not reachable from this environment. The acceptance table in
`DATA/round10.json` lets anyone apply them.
