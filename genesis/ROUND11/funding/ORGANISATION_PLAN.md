# G11-36 — Organisation plan

## Governance
* **Roles.** Principal investigator (host-institution staff member); project lead (scientific coordination; this project's author);
  technical lead for Proposal B; theory lead for Proposal A.
* **Steering.** Monthly meetings with minutes in the repository. Decisions on scope, publications and spending are recorded as issues
  or ADRs.
* **External review.** Each paper goes to at least one external reviewer (REVIEW_DOSSIER.md) before submission.

## Data management
* Code and processed data (JSON) are open in the repository. Event samples and raw detector data go to a DOI archive at publication.
* Raw data: HDF5 plus JSON metadata (G11-20), SHA-256 checksums, and a run logbook.
* Retention: as required by the funder and the host, typically ≥ 10 years.
* Every figure is regenerated by a script named in its caption or document.

## Safety
* G11-24 applies: HV interlocks, sealed-source licence via the host's radiation-safety officer (STUK supervision in Finland), and
  design review before first power.
* Training records are kept for every operator.

## Intellectual property
* **Default:** open source (code), open access (papers), open hardware for in-house boards (CERN-OHL family), subject to host policy.
* Third-party designs (e.g. community amplifier boards) are used under their own licences and are not relicensed.
* Any commercialisable element (e.g. front-end electronics) is declared to the host's innovation office before publication.

## Publication policy
* **Authorship:** substantial contribution plus approval of the final text (ICMJE-style). Collaboration members are listed by
  agreement.
* **Results labels** (Theorem / Proposition / Proposition-N / Assessment / Conjecture) are kept in papers. Failed approaches are
  documented.
* **No claim of discovery or formation of a daughter universe** without the gate in PAPER_C_OUTLINE.md.
* **Press:** none before peer review.
