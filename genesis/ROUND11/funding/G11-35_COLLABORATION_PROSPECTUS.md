# G11-35 — Collaboration prospectus (institutional)

**Purpose.** Find a host and partners for Proposals A and B. Nothing here implies that any institution has agreed.

| Partner type | What we ask | What we offer | Example (candidate, not contacted) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finnish timing-detector group | Host Proposal B; lend sensors, a probe station and a bonder; co-PI | Fully specified demonstrator; validated analysis software; student project | Helsinki Institute of Physics detector groups; university detector labs |
| Theory group (gravity / numerical relativity) | Host Proposal A; co-supervision | Tested characteristic code; Paper A draft; precise open problems | University theoretical-physics departments |
| Mathematical GR | Well-posedness and extension questions (O3, O4) | Concrete equations and numerical data | — |
| Sensor community (RD50 / DRD3) | Sensor samples; test-beam slots | Characterisation data back to the community | — |
| Collider phenomenology / FCC-hh studies | Review of Paper B; detector-simulation access | Signal generator and samples; reproducible configuration | — |

## First-contact package

Prospectus (G11-31), Paper A or B draft, review dossier, a one-page demonstrator summary (G11-17 table), and the repository link.

## Decision rule

Proceed with a proposal only once a host has confirmed in writing: PI eligibility, laboratory access, and the radiation-source licence
route.
