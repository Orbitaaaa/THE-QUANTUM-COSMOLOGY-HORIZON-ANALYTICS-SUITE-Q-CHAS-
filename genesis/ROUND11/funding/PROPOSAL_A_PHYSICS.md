# Proposal A — Fundamental physics: inner horizons and daughter spacetimes in pure-gravity regular black holes

## Objectives

1. **O1.** Decide whether generic D = 10 model-U interiors stay curvature-bounded at late times. Method: compactified-v
   characteristic code with mesh refinement, plus convergence at three resolutions. Addresses P-R11-a.
2. **O2.** Evolve inner-extremal towers after detuning, over times ≫ 1/κ₋. Determine whether a stable-core family of positive measure
   exists.
3. **O3.** Characterise the D = 5 weak null singularity: convergence of the Królak integral, and C⁰ / C¹ extendibility.
4. **O4.** Well-posedness of the tower off spherical symmetry (with mathematical-GR collaborators). Gravitational perturbations of the
   exterior.
5. **O5.** Papers A and C, and an open code release.

## Risks

* O1 may show late-time blow-up in D = 10. That is still a publishable negative result, and it closes the generic-daughter question.
* Lack of well-posedness is a theory risk, mitigated by staying in spherical symmetry for O1–O3.

## Expected outcome

A definite answer to: does any generic data set in this theory produce a daughter reached by the field equations?
