# Collaboration materials (G11-30)

## One-paragraph pitch for theorists

Pure-gravity regular black holes (quasi-topological towers) are attractive, but their inner horizons have not been evolved
nonlinearly with continuous fluxes. We have a tested characteristic code that does this. It finds a Tipler-weak null singularity in
D = 5, no mass inflation for inner-extremal towers, and range-limited evidence of curvature-bounded mass growth in D = 10. We seek
collaborators in numerical relativity (compactified evolution, mesh refinement) and in mathematical general relativity (well-posedness
of the tower; C⁰ extensions).

## One-paragraph pitch for experimentalists

Regular collider black holes would leave a cold, slow remnant. A hard jet recoils against missing energy, and if the remnant is charged
there is a slow, highly ionising track. We need 30–50 ps timing and multi-crossing readout. We propose a two-plane LGAD timing
demonstrator (bench Sr-90, then a beam test at 1 m) as the first hardware step, using existing facilities and sensors obtained through
collaboration.
