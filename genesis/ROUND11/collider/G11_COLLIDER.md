# Round 11 collider package (G11-09 … G11-16)

**Rule applied.** Every reach number below has a documented uncertainty budget and a reproducible configuration
(`ROUND11/collider/analysis_config.json`, `DATA/round11_manifest.json`).

| | Reach (FCC-hh 100 TeV, 30 ab⁻¹, n = 6, M_D(DL) = 8 TeV, Hayward, conservative trapping) |
|---|---|
| 5σ discovery | **M_ext = 33.4 (+0.8 / −1.1) TeV** |
| 95 % CL expected exclusion | **M_ext = 35.0 (+1.2 / −1.0) TeV** |

Total: quadrature sum of the one-at-a-time shifts below, positive and negative shifts separately (Proposition-N 11-8).

**Data, figure and code**
* Data: `DATA/round11_collider.json`.
* Figure: `FIGURES/round11_collider.png`.
* Code: `babyuniverse/collider11.py`, `greybody11.py`.
* Scripts: `scripts/round11a.py` (variation samples), `scripts/round11_greybody.py`, `scripts/round11b.py`.

**External vs internal.** Everything here is an exterior observable. A model-U hole whose Cauchy horizon is a weak null singularity
(G11-01) and one with a regular daughter (G11-02) have identical exteriors. A discovery would establish the tower: regular-BH
production with a stable, cold remnant and an onset at M_ext. It would **not** establish the daughter. That interpretation rests
entirely on the interior analysis (G11-01 … G11-04).

**Notes**
* **Robustness of the reach.** The reach is robust to the background because the signal cross-section falls by ~10× per 3 TeV of
  M_ext near the edge. Signal theory dominates, mainly the high-x parton luminosity: NNPDF3.1 gives 0.59× the NNPDF2.3 luminosity at
  c_l = 0.70.
* **Scale variations.** LO scale variations (±2–10 % on V+jets) understate missing higher orders. The ±20 % K-factor row covers the
  data-driven normalisation.
* **Smoothing.** Generator variations are applied as fitted ratios ln r = a + b ln(MET/TeV) to the nominal templates. Applying the
  raw 3000-event variation samples directly gives shifts of 0.0 to −0.8 TeV, dominated by MC fluctuations in the top MET bins. That
  is itself a finding: high-statistics tail samples are needed before a real analysis (P-R11-e).
* **EW corrections.** Weak corrections to Z+jet are negative and grow with p_T: up to ~20 % near 1 TeV
  ([Becher–Garcia i Tormo, arXiv:1305.4202](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4202)) and up to ~40 % at p_T ≳ 2 TeV at the LHC
  ([Kühn et al., hep-ph/0507178](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0507178)). Our LL weight 1 − 0.0059 ln²(MET²/M_W²) gives −15 % at 1 TeV
  and −24 % at 2 TeV, an estimate on the low side. It only reduces the background, so it is shown but not counted. The modern
  approach (NNLO QCD ⊗ NLO EW with control regions) is described by
  [Lindert et al., arXiv:1705.04664](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.04664).
* **Detector items not modelled.**
  * Timing: the 30–50 ps layer affects remnant identification, not the monojet reach.
  * Trigger: L1 MET efficiency is 99.9 % above 1 TeV (R217).
  * Reconstruction failures beyond the tail model.

  These are recorded as unresolved: they need a full detector simulation (FCC-hh Delphes or Geant4).

## G11-12 Greybody factors and the Hawking envelope

* **Method.** Brane fields see the induced 4D metric. Spin 0, ½ and 1 master equations are solved on the actual model-U f(r)
  (`greybody11`).
* **Validation.**
  * Schwarzschild agrees with the independent Round 6c code to 10⁻⁴.
  * Low-frequency Γ₀ → 4r₊²ω² (σ → horizon area).
  * High-frequency Σ(2j+1)Γ_j → (ωb_c)² to 1–2 %.
* **Proposition-N 11-6.**
  * Near extremality (μ = 1.05 μ_ext) gauge-boson emission falls to 7 % of the blackbody rate (scalars 142 %, fermions 101 %).
  * Total Standard Model luminosity: 0.92 of geometric at 1.05 μ_ext; 1.20–1.32 above 1.5 μ_ext; Tangherlini n = 6: 1.11.
  * The spin-1 share of emitted quanta drops from 26 % to 2 % near the remnant.
* **Collider effect.** Implemented as a generator patch (μ-dependent spectra and species weights). The reach changes by −0.06 TeV
  (discovery) and −0.19 TeV (exclusion). The Round 6o x³ spectrum turns out to be close to the computed greybody spectra; a plain
  geometric x² spectrum would have biased the reach by −1.6 TeV.
* **Not included.** Bulk graviton emission (extra missing energy; unresolved). Massive-particle greybody factors are approximated by
  Boltzmann suppression.

## G11-14 Remnant phenomenology

Sample s100_U_0.70 (M_rem ≈ 33.8 TeV).
* **Velocities.** β percentiles (5/25/50/75/95 %) = 0.02 / 0.06 / 0.11 / 0.18 / 0.30. Every remnant has β < 0.5.
* **Neutral remnant** (the default after colour discharge, R212): pure missing energy. This is what the reach above uses.
* **Singly charged remnant (Assessment):**
  * Median dE/dx ≈ 31 × MIP.
  * Time-of-flight delay at 1.5 m: median 41 ns, so 100 % arrive more than 1 ns late. Readout over many bunch crossings is required.
  * TOF mass resolution with 30 ps is 0.07 %, negligible against the tracker's p resolution at p ~ 4 TeV.
  * About 43 % would **stop** in 1450 g cm⁻² of iron-equivalent calorimeter. The depth is an assumption, not an FCC-hh CDR number.
    Bethe without shell corrections is unreliable below βγ ≈ 0.05, so this fraction is uncertain by roughly a factor of two.

  Stopped charged remnants are stable. Unlike R-hadron searches there is no late-decay signal; only the ionisation track and stopping
  point.
